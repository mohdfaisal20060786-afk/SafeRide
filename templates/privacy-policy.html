Privacy Policy

SafeRide values your privacy.

Information We Collect

SafeRide may collect information such as name, email address, phone number, emergency contact information, vehicle information, GPS location, accident information, and messages submitted through the contact form.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide account services, accident detection, location services, emergency alerts, and related safety features.

Location Information

SafeRide may use your device location to detect and save accident locations and provide emergency response features.

Account Deletion

Users can delete their account and associated information through the account deletion option available in the application.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact SafeRide through the contact information provided on the website.
