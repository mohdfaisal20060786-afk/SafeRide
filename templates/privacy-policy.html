Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

SafeRide ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SafeRide collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information when you use the SafeRide application and website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, SafeRide may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password information stored in protected/hashed form
  • Emergency contact name, phone number, and relationship
  • Vehicle information provided by the user
  • GPS latitude and longitude
  • Accident records and accident status
  • Emergency alert information
  • Messages submitted through the Contact Us form

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to provide and operate SafeRide features, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Displaying your profile information
  • Managing vehicle information
  • Managing emergency contacts
  • Detecting and recording accident events
  • Obtaining and displaying accident/current location
  • Generating emergency alert information
  • Maintaining accident history
  • Responding to Contact Us messages

3. Location Information

SafeRide requests access to your device's location when a location-based feature is used. Location information may include GPS latitude and longitude.

Location information is used to display your current location and, when an accident event is recorded, to associate the accident with its location.

SafeRide only requests location access when required for location-related functionality. You can manage location permissions through your device settings.

4. Emergency Contacts

You may voluntarily provide emergency contact information, including a contact's name, phone number, and relationship.

This information is used to prepare emergency alert information associated with an accident event.

5. Vehicle Information

If you provide vehicle details, SafeRide may store the vehicle number, model, and color associated with your account.

6. Accident Information

When an accident event is recorded, SafeRide may store information such as accident location, time, user account association, and accident status.

7. Emergency Alerts

SafeRide uses accident and emergency-contact information to generate emergency alert content and associate alerts with the relevant accident and user account.

The current SafeRide application generates and records emergency alert information within the service. Any external communication or messaging functionality is only used where specifically provided and enabled by the application.

8. Contact Form Information

When you submit the Contact Us form, the information you provide, such as your name, email address, subject, and message, may be stored so that we can respond to your inquiry.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or hosting providers that help us operate the SafeRide service, including hosting and database services. Such processing is limited to providing and maintaining the service.

10. Data Security

SafeRide uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Account passwords are stored using password hashing rather than being stored as plain-text passwords.

Data transmitted between the application and the online service is intended to use HTTPS encryption.

11. Data Retention

We retain account and related information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SafeRide features, maintain records, resolve issues, or comply with legal obligations.

12. Account and Data Deletion

SafeRide provides an account deletion option within the application/profile area.

When an account deletion request is completed, associated account information and related application data are deleted from the SafeRide database, subject to any data that may need to be retained where legally required.

Users may also contact SafeRide regarding account or data deletion using the privacy contact information below.

13. Children's Privacy

SafeRide is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

14. Third-Party Services

SafeRide may use third-party infrastructure and services required to host, store, or operate the application. These services may process information on our behalf.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to SafeRide, legal requirements, or data practices. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
